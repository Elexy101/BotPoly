How to Play BOTPOLY

  1. Connect MetaMask to BOT Chain Testnet (Chain ID 968). Claim tBOT and tUSDT from the BOT Chain Faucet.
  2. Mint HPOLY – one-time 100 HPOLY starter tokens.
  3. Start Game – lock your starting balance.
  4. Roll Dice – move around 12 tiles. PROFIT tiles earn HPOLY, LOSS tiles burn it.
  5. Buy Boosts – spend Test USDT on Rerolls or Double Rewards.
  6. Claim Point – burn the required HPOLY to earn a permanent point.
  7. End Game – stop playing and release your state.
